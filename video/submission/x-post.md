## Why both links, and why this shape

- **Both links stay.** `HACKATHON.md` §2 asks the post to "link the demo and public repo". The repo is
  what a judge or a screening agent opens first, and the hosted demo is the next step for someone who
  has never heard of an enclave. Two links cost 46 of 280 characters; a post that keeps only the repo
  asks a non-technical reader to go hunting inside a codebase, and one that keeps only the demo cannot
  be reviewed.
- **First line is the problem, not the product.** No blind/low-vision statistic in line one: a stranger
  scrolling needs the human situation in five words before a number.
- **One complete moment is the video**, not the text: letter → enclave → the reading → the quoted
  answer → the receipt. The text only says what came back (signed answers, the check list, 0 KB).
- **NEAR's role is concrete**: the enclave that does the inference is on NEAR AI Cloud, which is why
  the answer can be verified instead of trusted. No "powered by NEAR".
- **The next step is a link, not a question** — a question in a 280-character post invites replies
  nobody is watching at submission time; the repo and demo are what judging needs.
- **Nothing unproven is claimed**: no accuracy figure, no "end-to-end encrypted", no "verified" total.
  "0 KB kept" is the app's own statement; "checks listed" is the receipt's behaviour, gaps included.
